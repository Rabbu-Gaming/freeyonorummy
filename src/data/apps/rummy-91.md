### Understand Rummy Rules

Rummy is a card game focused on forming valid sets and sequences from 13 cards.

The basic gameplay works as follows:

- Each player is dealt 13 cards at the start of a round.
- On each turn, a player draws one card from the open or closed deck and discards one.
- The goal is to form valid **sets** (3+ cards of the same rank) and **sequences** (3+ consecutive cards of the same suit).
- A valid declaration requires at least two sequences, one of which must be a pure sequence with no joker.
- The first player to complete a valid declaration wins the round.

### Tips for New Players

- Always form a pure sequence first before building other combinations.
- Avoid holding high-value cards (K, Q, J) too long as they increase penalty points.
- Observe your opponents' discards to estimate their hands.
- Start with lower-stake tables to gain experience.
