### Understand Rummy Rules

Rummy is played by forming valid combinations of cards.

The basic gameplay works as follows:

- Each player receives 13 cards at the start of a round.
- Players draw one card from the open or closed deck on each turn.
- Players must discard one card after drawing.
- The goal is to form valid **sets** (3 or more cards of the same rank) and **sequences** (3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit).
- A valid declaration requires at least two sequences, one of which must be a pure sequence (no joker).
- The first player to complete a valid declaration wins the round.

### Tips for New Players

- Focus on forming a pure sequence before anything else.
- Avoid holding high-value cards for too long as they increase your penalty points.
- Observe which cards your opponents discard to estimate their hand.
- Start with lower-stake tables to gain experience.
- Verify your account early to avoid delays when withdrawing.
