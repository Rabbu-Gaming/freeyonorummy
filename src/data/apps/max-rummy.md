### Rummy Basics

If you're new to rummy, the short version is: arrange your cards into valid sequences and sets, with at least one pure sequence required in most formats.

- **Pure Sequence:** consecutive cards, same suit, no joker
- **Impure Sequence:** consecutive cards, same suit, joker allowed
- **Set:** three or more same-value cards across different suits
- **Declaration:** completing the required combinations and declaring per the table's rules

Scoring and format details vary by table, so it's worth reading the in-app rules before joining anything beyond a free table.
